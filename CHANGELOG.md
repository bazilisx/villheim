# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First playable version, not yet tested in game.

- Village sites in the Meadows and Black Forest, placed by the world generator.
- Villages grow over in-game days by copying buildings from existing locations (vanilla or from other mods).
- Villagers built from the vanilla player model, with vanilla hair, beards, clothes and tools.
- Day and night routine, greetings and chatter, and a village summary on the hearth.
- Console commands for founding, growing, previewing and inspecting designs.
