## Session 2, 21:00: site chest, biome materials, movement

### What Got Done
- Read the VikingColonies 1.6.0 source and a decompiled Innangard 0.4.0. Wrote `docs/LEARNINGS.md` with the ideas
  taken (walking, stand spots, builder ring, grid-searched roads, newcomers for free beds, free time).
- Site chest (`Village/SiteChest.cs`): a wood chest is placed beside every building site. The village stock moves
  into it, villagers carry what they gather to it, the builder pays each piece from it, and players can add to it.
  When the building finishes, leftover materials go back to the stock and the chest is removed if empty.
- `Village/VillageNeeds.cs`: what the village is short of. With a site, the chest's shortfall for the rest of the
  building. Between buildings, a stock target per biome material. Nobody harvests when nothing is short.
- One builder per village (`Chores.IsVillageBuilder`): the villager with the builder trade, otherwise the lowest
  seed. Builds whenever the chest covers the next piece; gathers otherwise. Hammers each piece for
  `SecondsPerPiece` (2.5 s) from a ring outside the building.
- Biome materials (`Materials.AllowedIn`, config `BiomeMaterials`): designs that need a material the biome lacks are
  dropped from that biome's pools. Default: Meadows has wood, fine wood and stone; Black Forest has wood, core wood
  and stone.
- `Villagers/Walker.cs`: shared movement with steady goals, two-distance arrival, jump after 3 s stuck, sidestep
  after 5 s, give up after 3 sidesteps.
- Stand spots: trees are cut from the hearth side so they fall away from the village; logs, stumps and rocks are
  worked from the near side. Worked out once per target.
- `Villagers/Yields.cs`: reads drop tables so villagers pick trees that give the material that is short (pines for
  core wood, birch and oak for fine wood).
- Passive income while unloaded is split by what the site still needs.
- Design report gained a materials column.
- Handoff docs: `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/SESSION_HISTORY.md`, `docs/ROADMAP.md`, `docs/DEVELOPMENT.md`, daily notes.

## Session 3, 22:00: test results for the site chest build

### Tested In Game
- Overall: villagers gather and the builder builds the house from the site chest. Movement still stutters.
- Issue 1: the builder hammers with the axe. She should hold the hammer while building and the right tool for each
  job (axe for trees, pickaxe for rocks). The designated builder can be a woodcutter or miner by trade
  (`Chores.IsVillageBuilder` falls back to the lowest seed), and nothing re-equips tools when the job changes.
- Issue 2: with the site chest open in the inventory screen, the wood count does not go down while pieces are
  placed. With the chest closed it goes down. Possible causes: `SiteChest.Take` returns false while
  `Container.IsInUse()` and the site falls back to another path, or the open container UI does not refresh after a
  change made by code. Needs checking in `ConstructionSite.Pay` and `SiteChest.Take`.
- Issue 3: the second villager does nothing, pushes into the player, and jitters in place.
- Issue 4: the builder ended up inside the house with the door closed and keeps jumping (the Walker's unstuck jump).
  Villagers need to open doors (Door.Interact) or the builder needs to stay outside: the stand ring is outside the
  footprint, but she still got in.
- Issue 5: a wall above the door went up before anything held it and broke. Build order needs support awareness:
  walls touching the ground first, then walls touching those, then walls and roofs touching those. Doors and windows
  go in before any wall or roof that rests on them. The current order is stage first (structure, roof, openings,
  interior), then height, so openings come after the walls above them.
- Issue 6: a copied design included a bee hive (`Beehive`). Bee hives must never be built. Add it to the default
  `ExcludedObjects` or a hard-coded exclusion in `DesignSpawner.ExclusionReason`.

### What's Still In Progress
- All six issues above are open. The session ended here at the daily usage limit.

## Session 4, 22:00: fixes for the six test issues

### What Got Done
- Issue 1 (tools): every villager now carries an axe, a pickaxe and a hammer for their gear tier
  (`VillagerLooks.Apply`). `VillagerLooks.Hold` puts the right one in hand: axe for trees and logs, pickaxe for
  rocks, hammer while building. They start out holding their trade's tool.
- Issue 2 (open site chest): `SiteChest.Usable` now allows changes to an open chest when this machine owns it, which
  is the case when the local player opened it. A chest open on another machine still waits. Verbose logging prints
  each take with the count left and whether the chest was open. The old code refused every change while the chest
  was open, so the builder hammered without placing anything.
- Issue 3 (second villager idle, pushing, jitter): two causes found. A villager whose tool did not match the needed
  material could only pick up drops and pickables, so with neither around it did nothing; everyone can now fell and
  mine. Idle villagers were left to the game's own wandering around their patrol point, which is the hearth fire or
  the middle of their house. New `Villagers/Loiter.cs` replaces it: walk to a spot near home (near the hearth at
  night), stand 8 to 20 s, pick another. Spots keep clear of the fire, buildings, the building site and players. A
  player within 1 m makes the villager move off. Walking villagers wait up to 3 s for a player in the way, then step
  around.
- Enemies: villagers now hand over to the game AI whenever `BaseAI.FindEnemy` sees an enemy (checked once a second),
  so chores and idling no longer keep them from noticing monsters.
- Issue 4 (trapped behind a door): the builder stopped wherever they were once within 6 m of the next piece, which could
  be inside the outline. The builder now walks out of the footprint before building. `Walker` opens doors: after 1 s without
  moving it looks for an unlocked door within 4 m with the villager on one side and the goal on the other, walks to
  it, opens it, steps through and closes it once 2.2 m clear.
- Issue 5 (support): new `Designs/BuildOrder.cs`. The shell (pieces from the hammer's building tabs plus doors and
  windows) goes up so every piece is held: on the ground, on top of a placed piece with real overlap, or beside one
  for at least 0.5 m of height. Lowest ready piece first. Furniture and other pieces come after the shell. Sizes
  come from each prefab's colliders. Checked off-game with stubbed Unity types on a wall row with a door, a piece
  above the door, an upper row and a two-piece roof: the door goes in before the piece above it.
- Sites begun before this change keep the old order (new hearth key `villheim_site_order`), so the piece index of a
  house in progress still lines up.
- Issue 6 (bee hives): `DesignSpawner.ExclusionReason` drops anything with a `Beehive` component.
- `scripts/build-cloud.sh`: compile check for cloud sessions that cannot reach nuget.bepinex.dev.
