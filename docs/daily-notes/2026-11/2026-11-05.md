## Session 2, 21:00: site chest, biome materials, movement

### What Got Done
- Read the VikingColonies 1.6.0 source and a decompiled Innangard 0.4.0. Wrote `docs/LEARNINGS.md` with the ideas
  taken (walking, stand spots, builder ring, grid-searched roads, newcomers for free beds, free time).
- Site chest (`Village/SiteChest.cs`): a wood chest is placed beside every building site. The village stock moves
  into it, villagers carry what they gather to it, the builder pays each piece from it, and players can add to it.
  When the building finishes, leftover materials go back to the stock and the chest is removed if empty.
- `Village/VillageNeeds.cs`: what the village is short of. With a site, the chest's shortfall for the rest of the
  building. Between buildings, a stock target per biome material. Nobody harvests when nothing is short.
- One builder per village (`Chores.IsVillageBuilder`): the villager with the builder trade, otherwise the lowest
  seed. Builds whenever the chest covers the next piece; gathers otherwise. Hammers each piece for
  `SecondsPerPiece` (2.5 s) from a ring outside the building.
- Biome materials (`Materials.AllowedIn`, config `BiomeMaterials`): designs that need a material the biome lacks are
  dropped from that biome's pools. Default: Meadows has wood, fine wood and stone; Black Forest has wood, core wood
  and stone.
- `Villagers/Walker.cs`: shared movement with steady goals, two-distance arrival, jump after 3 s stuck, sidestep
  after 5 s, give up after 3 sidesteps.
- Stand spots: trees are cut from the hearth side so they fall away from the village; logs, stumps and rocks are
  worked from the near side. Worked out once per target.
- `Villagers/Yields.cs`: reads drop tables so villagers pick trees that give the material that is short (pines for
  core wood, birch and oak for fine wood).
- Passive income while unloaded is split by what the site still needs.
- Design report gained a materials column.
- Handoff docs: `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/SESSION_HISTORY.md`, `docs/ROADMAP.md`, `docs/DEVELOPMENT.md`, daily notes.
