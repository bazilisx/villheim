## Session 1, 00:00 Mountain Time: overnight work through the roadmap

Tyler asked for as much of the roadmap as possible overnight with few agents, leaning on Millénaire for the design.
Everything below compiles with `scripts/build-cloud.sh`; nothing was tested in game. Each commit went to main, so the
updater installs the latest logic build. No core (`src/Villheim`) change, so no game restart is needed beyond the
usual hot reload.

### What Got Done
- **Building** (`a03aa3f`):
  - Pieces only go up while the builder is next to them. The slow unattended placement (one piece per hearth tick
    when no builder had swung recently) was the cause of pieces appearing 15 m from a builder busy fighting. Time
    spent unloaded is still made up on load.
  - The builder stands a step out from the next piece's edge (outward from the building's middle) and stays put while
    he hammers. Before, he walked to a ring outside the whole building and the walk and stop calls alternated every
    frame while he was near, which is the likely cause of the jitter at work.
  - The whole outline is levelled when ground is broken, and the ground under each floor or bottom wall piece is
    levelled to its base before it goes up (the stone pieces that broke in the last test were in the air).
  - Punching: `GetCurrentWeapon` returns the unarmed "weapon" when the hand is empty, so a villager whose tool went
    missing punched trees, pieces and the workbench. A missing tool is now handed over again, and tool animations
    only play when the right tool is in hand.
  - Anything named like a hive is left out of designs, on top of the Beehive component check.
- **Beds, sleep, placement** (`4129c76`):
  - Each villager claims a bed (stored on him by position) and sleeps in it from a bedtime between 22:00 and 23:00
    to a wake time between 05:00 and 06:00, fixed per villager. An enemy wakes him. Lying down holds him at the bed's
    spawn point with the `attach_bed` animator bool.
  - Capacity is one villager per bed, never fewer than the first settlers. Newcomers come only for a free bed, by
    day, walking in from 40 m out.
  - Finished buildings get furnished from the village chest: the longhouse a bed per first settler, a small home
    (radius under 9 m) one bed, a larger home two, a workshop its workbench. The spot must pass the game's own cover
    test (`Cover.GetCoverForPoint`, under a roof, cover 0.7 or more), be on the ground floor, clear of doors, with
    room in front, back to a wall where possible. The outdoor workbench is gone from the hearth ring.
  - Placement after Millénaire's `BuildingLocationFinder`: distance bands per role (longhouse 0 to 0.35 of the
    village radius, homes 0.1 to 0.65, workshops 0.3 to 0.9, farms 0.45 to 1), ring order from the inside out,
    nothing past the radius, spots refused on or across any path, workshops pick the spot with the most trees and
    rocks within 25 m.
- **Cooking** (`3f71658`): a hurt villager eats cooked food from the chest, or takes raw meat or fish to the cooking
  station over the hearth, puts it on (`CookItem`), sits beside the fire for the food's own `m_cookTime`, takes it off
  (clears the slot) and eats it. Food heals like a player's: regen value every 10 s for the food's duration, up to
  three foods. `FoodHealing` in the config scales it. No food at all: roasted scraps heal 2% every 10 s for five
  minutes. In free time one villager keeps up to six cooked meals in the chest.
- **Roles** (`e4fba45`): protector and carer by arrival order. Protectors take a club or spear and shield in a fight;
  carers flee while a protector is there. Idle protectors keep watch at the village edge, carers sit by the fire and
  cook. From six villagers, trades by arrival order: carpenter (the builder), woodcutter, miner, cook. Under six,
  everyone works the material with the biggest need.
- **Dialogue** (`15759ff`): topics with conditions on the moment (activity, health, needs, building, beds, trade,
  role, time, rain, village size). Used when talking to a villager and now and then on their own near a player.
- **Paths** (`68879d9`): a finished building's path is queued on the hearth. One idle villager at a time walks it with
  a hoe and lays it a metre at a time; progress survives fights, night and logouts.
- **Millénaire study**: decompiled the whole jar (737 files) and had one background agent write a design summary.
  The findings are in `docs/LEARNINGS.md`, how to decompile again in `docs/DEVELOPMENT.md`.

### Decisions Made
- Roles never depend on looks or gender. Millénaire ties trades to gender in its Norman culture; Villheim does not.
- Building materials are still paid per piece (Millénaire takes the whole bill at once).
- Food heals by the player's rule (regen every 10 s for the food's duration).
