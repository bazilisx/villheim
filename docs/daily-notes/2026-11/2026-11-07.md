## Session 4: pass 2 and material swaps

### What Got Done
- Pass 1 done by Tyler: 264 of 963 designs kept (263 "Yes w/ edits", 1 "Yes"); the rest default to No.
  Meadows 127 (90 homes), Swamp 66, Black Forest 39, later stages 32.
- `Village/Keepers.cs`: the 264 kept design names, taken from the sheet.
- `vh gallery keepers [as <stage>] [page N]`: only the keepers, kind by kind (homes, halls, workshops, storehouses,
  farms), each kind starting a new page: 29 pages, the same pages as the Pass 2 tab. `as meadows` (or any stage)
  shows each design with its building pieces swapped for that stage's; the sign says how many were swapped and kept.
- `Village/MaterialSwap.cs`: same-shape swaps. A piece matches another of the same kind in its name (wall, roof,
  floor, beam, pole, door, window...) and the same collider size within a few cm, either way round; the new piece is
  put where the old one's middle was, so differing pivots line up. Down a stage, the newest piece the target stage
  has; up a stage, only a newer piece of the same shape. No match: the piece stays. Decoration is never swapped.
- `vh bp swap <number|name> <stage>`: saves a swapped copy to `blueprints/inspiration/edits/<name>_as_<stage>`
  (review only, villages do not build it); `vh gallery show <that name>` shows it.
- Gallery locations are laid out without the build order (faster).
- Review sheet: Pass 2 tab (first tab): page, number, name, kind, built-as stage, Use for (pre-filled from the pass 1
  notes and kind; workshops left blank), tick boxes for Meadows, Black Forest, Swamp, Mountain, Plains, village
  level, note, link. Top bar picks the stage to show as and gives the in-game command. Script updated for both tabs.

  Goblin roof angles ("45d") read as 45.
- `vh look` (Village/LookAt.cs): names the object under the crosshair within 4 m: prefab, kind, size, stage and
  what the swap makes of it for each earlier stage; each new name goes to BepInEx/Villheim/looked-at.txt.
- Tyler wants to choose stand-ins himself (the goblin wood wall became the round-pole fence, not what he had in
  mind). `vh swap` (Village/SwapTool.cs): aim at a gallery piece, press middle mouse (config GallerySwapKey); a row of
  the likeliest stand-ins goes up in front of the player; aim at one (or any piece built by hand) and press again.
  Every copy of the original within 40 m (GallerySwapRadius) is replaced, middle on middle, and the pairing is saved
  to BepInEx/Villheim/swap-pairs.txt, used by later pages and `vh bp swap` for that stage and the ones after.
  `vh swap turn` gives the last pairing a quarter turn. Stand-ins now carry the original's name and rotation in
  their ZDO, so picking a stand-in picks what it stands in for; `vh look` shows it too.
- `vh note <text>`: appends to BepInEx/Villheim/notes.txt with the time, what the crosshair is on (and what it
  stands in for) and the nearest gallery sign.
- Swap reworked to Tyler's design, piece by piece and design by design: keepers pages go up as built (`as <stage>`
  still works but is not the way to review). `vh swap` on, middle-click a piece of a design: stand-ins for that
  piece go up 3 m in front of the design's sign, in groups by biome (config GallerySwapBiomes, default Meadows,
  Black Forest, Swamp; up to 4 each, same kind first, nearest size), each group behind a label sign. Middle-click
  a stand-in (or any hand-built piece): every piece of that kind in that design only changes to it. Choices are kept
  per design in BepInEx/Villheim/design-swaps.txt and come back when the design is put up again. `vh swap turn`,
  `vh swap undo`, `vh swap done`. Gallery pieces and signs carry their design's name in the ZDO. Keepers rows have
  a 14 m aisle for the stand-ins. `vh bp save <number>` writes a design with its chosen stand-ins as a blueprint.
- Group stand-ins (Tyler: the dvergr wall is twice as wide as the fence he wants): middle-clicking a piece built by
  hand takes it and every hand-built piece touching it (up to 20) as one stand-in, kept relative to the group's
  middle and the clicked piece's turn ("group[prefab@x,y,z,qx,qy,qz,qw;...]" in design-swaps.txt). Each original is
  replaced by the whole group, middle on middle. Stand-in pieces now carry their original's middle
  (villheim_swapped_center), so the pieces of one group count as one original when swapped again. `vh bp save`
  writes every piece of a group.
- Hand edits in the gallery were not kept (only swap choices were). Each design's front sign now holds its plot
  (middle, half width, turn), number and how many pieces went up. `vh bp save <number>` saves what stands on the
  plot now: the design's pieces that are left, its stand-ins and anything built by hand inside the plot; removed
  pieces are simply absent. `vh bp save page` saves every changed design standing near the player (`all` for every
  one). Saves: blueprints/inspiration/pass3/p3-<number>-<name>.blueprint, listed in BepInEx/Villheim/pass3-saved.csv.
  The gallery puts a design up from its pass 3 save from then on (sign: "edited (pass 3 save)"). Pass 3 and edit
  copies are left out of `vh report`. These saves are the input to pass 3 (decor tiers a1/a2/a3).
- Swaps came back after clearing the page (Tyler did not want that): choices were kept in design-swaps.txt and put
  in on every rebuild. Now a swap lives only in the world until the design is saved (`vh bp save <number>` or
  `vh bp save page`); a page put up again without saving shows the originals. The old design-swaps.txt is moved to
  design-swaps-unused.txt once. `vh bp save` of a design needs it standing nearby. `vh bp forget <number|name|all>`
  deletes pass 3 saves.
- Combination stand-ins (Tyler): where both names give a size ("2x2"), combinations of one smaller piece of the same
  kind that make up the size are offered too, up to 2 per biome: two stone_wall_2x1 stacked for a 2x2 wall, four
  1x1. 2 to 8 pieces, each side within a few cm, thickness free. A click on any piece of a combination takes all.
- Nudging (Tyler: some designs hover because their lowest pieces are meant to sit in the ground): with `vh swap` on,
  Page Up/Down move the design under the crosshair (or the last picked) up/down, the arrow keys left/right/away/
  towards as the player looks; 10 cm a press, 50 cm with Shift; stand-ins and hand-built pieces on the plot move
  too. The total is kept on the front sign; `vh bp save` writes the height as "#GroundOffset:" in the pass 3 file,
  and the gallery applies it after setting the design on its foundation (Blueprints.Blueprint.GroundOffset).
- Nudge keys moved to the numpad (Tyler has no Page Up/Down): + and - up and down, 8 2 4 6 away, towards, left,
  right (arrows and Page Up/Down still work). Holding Ctrl tints the structure under the crosshair green (the piece
  and every piece of the design touching it, found with a 2 m grid; MaterialMan emission) and the numpad then moves
  only that structure (a hut beside a hall). The front sign is marked touched, so `vh bp save page` takes it.
