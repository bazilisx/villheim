## Session 3, 19:00 Mountain Time: every design listed, one stage each

### What Got Done
- Why only 293 of 911 were listed: the old stage test dropped a design when any single piece (a tar lantern, an iron
  nail beam, a goblin totem) needed a later material or station, or when it had fewer than 15 building pieces.
- Now nothing is dropped. Each design gets one stage: the first stage whose materials and stations cover all but one
  in ten of its building pieces (walls, floors, beams, roofs, doors, windows). Decoration, furniture and crafting
  stations never set the stage. Later-stage pieces, pieces a player can't build and pieces from mods that are not
  installed are named in a Notes column. Fine wood, core wood and coal count as Black Forest (bronze axe, kiln).
  Stages go up to Ashlands.
- `vh report` writes `BepInEx/Villheim/design-list.csv`: every curated design, every blueprint (inspiration folders
  included) and every location with at least 5 building pieces, sorted by stage, kind and size, numbered, ten to a
  page. `vh report rescan` opens every location again; `vh report locations` is the old per-location report. The lists
  no longer run by themselves on world load.
- `vh gallery page N` shows page N of that list; `vh gallery <stage> [kind] [page N]` shows one stage. Signs show the
  stage and the design's number from the list.
- Review sheet: Designs tab reshaped (Stage, Building pieces, Mod notes; Verdict is Use as-is / Use after edits / Not
  usable; Use for; Village level; Your notes). New Variants tab for the family naming (meadowshouse1_a1).
- Roadmap: building families and variants, villagers adding decoration piece by piece between upgrades, shipping
  finished blueprints inside the mod.

## Session 4: pass 2 and material swaps

### What Got Done
- Pass 1 done by Tyler: 264 of 963 designs kept (263 "Yes w/ edits", 1 "Yes"); the rest default to No.
  Meadows 127 (90 homes), Swamp 66, Black Forest 39, later stages 32.
- `Village/Keepers.cs`: the 264 kept design names, taken from the sheet.
- `vh gallery keepers [as <stage>] [page N]`: only the keepers, kind by kind (homes, halls, workshops, storehouses,
  farms), each kind starting a new page: 29 pages, the same pages as the Pass 2 tab. `as meadows` (or any stage)
  shows each design with its building pieces swapped for that stage's; the sign says how many were swapped and kept.
- `Village/MaterialSwap.cs`: same-shape swaps. A piece matches another of the same kind in its name (wall, roof,
  floor, beam, pole, door, window...) and the same collider size within a few cm, either way round; the new piece is
  put where the old one's middle was, so differing pivots line up. Down a stage, the newest piece the target stage
  has; up a stage, only a newer piece of the same shape. No match: the piece stays. Decoration is never swapped.
- `vh bp swap <number|name> <stage>`: saves a swapped copy to `blueprints/inspiration/edits/<name>_as_<stage>`
  (review only, villages do not build it); `vh gallery show <that name>` shows it.
- Gallery locations are laid out without the build order (faster).
- Review sheet: Pass 2 tab (first tab): page, number, name, kind, built-as stage, Use for (pre-filled from the pass 1
  notes and kind; workshops left blank), tick boxes for Meadows, Black Forest, Swamp, Mountain, Plains, village
  level, note, link. Top bar picks the stage to show as and gives the in-game command. Script updated for both tabs.
