**Cluster B: lag (80c03c2, b002bc9, 5d5c7d2).**
- B1 `PieceIndex`: one list of every loaded piece in 16 m cells. Built at most once a frame, and only after a piece
  was made or removed (postfixes on `Piece.Awake` and `Piece.OnDestroy`) or after 5 s. All village callers of
  `Piece.GetAllPiecesInRadius` use it (Repairs.Present, Smithing, ConstructionSite, MapTables, Ruins, Palisade x4,
  PlotFinder x3, Demisters, Farms, HearthGround x2, WorldLife, Trails, HouseSigns). Setting `PieceIndex` off goes
  back to the game's call. `HouseSigns.FrontDoor` (kept 10 s per home), `StorageChests.Of` and
  `Interior.CountInside` (kept 2 s) read the list in place of spheres of colliders.
- B2 Blueprint folders read on a worker thread: at logic load (so the first plan after a load or reload does not wait
  on the disk) and every two minutes. Setting `BlueprintsInBackground`.
- B3 `LagProbe`: with LagLog on, a "Lag summary" line every `LagSummarySeconds` (60): frame times (average, slowest
  5 % and 1 %, hitches over 50 ms) and milliseconds per frame by part. Parts: every `Perf.Time` step, the hearth tick,
  the villagers' update, the brain before, during (by the doer that took the turn) and after the work chain, and the
  game's work for villagers (VillagerAI.UpdateAI, Humanoid.CustomFixedUpdate, VisEquipment.UpdateVisuals, gear
  models made in AttachItem, ZNetScene.CreateObject by kind). Gear models and spawns over LagLogMs are named one by
  one, which shows what is worth loading ahead of time.
- B3 `vh lag` commands for the draw cost that code cannot time (shaders, clothes, tools, hair):
  `vh lag hide villagers|gear|animals [s]`, `vh lag shadows [s]`, `vh lag ab <on/off setting> [s]` (for example
  `vh lag ab GhostLook`), `vh lag looks` (parts, materials, see-through materials, vertices and shadow casters per
  villager), `vh lag stop`.
- B3 notice board: its lines worked out once per update (Stats and the plain board each did it, each running the
  next-step search); headings assigned only when the board's signs change or every 5 minutes. Living-village hearth
  parts timed one by one ("Living.prosperity" and so on). The ghost look reuses one renderer list.
