# Community builds, as the lab draws them

These sheets show community blueprints from valheimians.com drawn by our lab renderer (the same low-poly models as our own designs). They are here to study and compare against, as Tyler decided in D-063. Every build belongs to its creator; credit and the build's own pictures are on valheimians.com. The lab learns measurements and motifs from them; no build is copied into a village.

- top-downloads: the 48 most downloaded vanilla builds in the corpus.
- homes: 48 of the most downloaded houses.
