# ------------------------------------------------------------------ events

@ self, event:feast
I've eaten so many berries I can't feel my teeth.
Good year. Remember it when the next one isn't.
Somebody save me a mushroom. Just one.

@ talk, event:feast
We had a feast. You missed it. There's maybe a handful of berries left if you're quick.
The harvest came in heavy. Everyone's in a good mood, so ask for favours now.

@ self, event:skald
I can still hear that song. All of it. Over and over.
The skald said the sea serpent was this long. I think he was lying.

@ talk, event:skald
A skald came through and sang half the night. My throat hurts and I only listened.
Did you hear the skald? He sang about a king who lost his boots. It was better than it sounds.

@ talk, event:fair

Market at {neighbour}. I came back with a comb and no idea why.

@ talk, event:takenin
Someone found a traveller lost in the woods. They're staying. We'll see how that goes.
We've a new face at the fire. Found wandering, half starved. Be kind to them.

@ self, event:sickness
Keep your cup to yourself, there's fever about.
I'm not sick. I'm just tired. That's different.

@ talk, event:sickness
There's fever in the village. Don't stand too close to anyone coughing.
One of ours is down with a fever. Rest and broth, that's all you can do.

@ self, event:beasts
Did you hear them last night? Right at the edge of the trees.
I didn't sleep a wink. Every branch was a wolf.

@ talk, event:beasts
Something was prowling round the houses last night. Keep a weapon by you.
We kept the fire high all night. Whatever it was, it didn't like the light.

@ self, event:quarrel
Some people need to learn to say sorry.
I'm not taking sides. I'm just saying who's right.

@ talk, event:quarrel
Two of ours had words. Don't mention it, they'll both want you on their side.

@ talk, event:dispute
We've fallen out with {neighbour}. Don't ask me what about, nobody agrees.
Things are cold with {neighbour} just now. It'll pass. Probably.

@ talk, event:cargo
