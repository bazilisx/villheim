# Villheim villager lines: what they say when you talk to them (talk) and on their own when you are near (self).
#
# A section starts with "@" and a list of tags that must all hold. "!tag" means the tag must not hold.
# After "|" come the voices the section is for (steady, gruff, cheerful, worrier, wry, elder, young, devout),
# separated by "/". After "*" a weight (default 1). Sections with more tags are picked more often when they fit.
# A section with no channel tag is used for both talk and self. "talk" or "self" limits it to one.
#
# Tags:
#   doing:felling splitting mining building carrying gathering cooking fetching roasting watch path making fire
#         stroll chatting station heading idle
#   healthy hurt badlyhurt anyhurt fed unfed

#   building:any building:longhouse building:home building:workshop building:farm
#   freebed nobed   mood:high mood:fair mood:low   afterfight   food:cooked food:raw food:none
#
# Fill-ins: {village} {material} {building} {name} {player} {other}
#
