# ------------------------------------------------------------------ the forge

@ at:forge

Hot work, this. You can feel it on your face from here.
Keep your sleeves away from the coals.
Copper and tin. Two stones that are no use alone and good together. Like some marriages.
When the bellows sigh like that, the fire is happy.
Mind the anvil, it bites toes.

The forge doesn't care how tired you are.

@ at:forge | cheerful

@ at:forge | worrier

@ at:forge | wry

@ at:forge | elder

@ at:forge | young

@ at:forge | devout

@ at:forge, skill:crafting:good
