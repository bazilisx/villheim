# Villheim: answers. "reply" lines are said by a villager who overheard another one (tone:joke, tone:happy,
# tone:sad, tone:worry, tone:angry, tone:proud, tone:point, tone:pious), or who saw the player make a gesture
# (emote:wave and so on). Plus lines for being sent to a player or a new building:
# event:sitwithplayer event:playerhurt event:latevisit event:viewbuilding event:welcomeneighbour

@ reply, tone:joke
