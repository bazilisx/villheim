# Villheim: what villagers know of the land round them. Same format as lines.txt. Every line here is about a real
# place the village has found (Village/Knowledge.cs, Village/Rumors.cs); nothing in it may name a place or a creature
# that the tags don't vouch for.
#
# Tags: rumor, rumor:saw (he saw it himself), rumor:heard (someone told him in a chat), rumor:common (the whole
# village has heard by now), rumor:<kind>, rumor:<kind>:<key>, rumor:near (under 350 m), rumor:far (over 900 m),
# rumor:spotted (seen from a way off: only animal grounds and biome edges), rumor:new (he found it today),
# rumor:felled (a boss that is down), rumor:when:day/night/any, rumor:sure (a runestone with boar, Greydwarfs or Draugr
# always around it: say it with confidence), rumor:ground (an animal ground or biome edge, not a place: hedge it),
# rumor:terrain:sea/river/mountain/ridge/swamp/forest/hill/high/down/shore/flat. Chat: tell:rumor (he is passing it on).
# Kinds and keys: boss eikthyr elder bonemass moder yagluth queen fader; dungeon burial sunkencrypt trollcave frostcave
# infestedmine; trader haldor hildir bogwitch; runestone runestone boars greydwarfs draugr; landmark stones tarpit tower
# giant henge drakenest (dolmen wreck well cairn greydwarfcamp only if KnowledgeKinds lists them); fuling; dvergr; ore
# copper tin silver obsidian flametal; forage beehive; game boar deer neck hare lox; foe (creatures that fight); biome
# meadows blackforest swamp mountain plains ocean mistlands ashlands deepnorth. Ruins, nests and berries are off by
# default (KnowledgeKinds).
#
# Fill-ins (Village/Rumors.cs; each rolls a different short shape every time it is used):
#   {thing}      what it is, with the game's names: "a troll cave", "Eikthyr's altar", "a runestone with boar all around it"
#   {thingshort} "the troll cave", "the boar runestone"
#   {where}      direction and maybe distance or a known place on the way: "to the north", "north, a fair walk",
#                "over the ridge to the north", "past the tar pit"; under 150 m "just past the north fence"
#   {direction}  "to the north", "north of here", "out north", "off north"
#   {dir}        the bare compass word: "north", "northeast"
#   {terrain}    "over the ridge", "across the river"..., empty on flat ground (spaces close up)
#   {via}        a known place on the way ("past the tar pit"), else {terrain}, else "out"
#   {distance}   "a short walk", "a fair walk", "a long walk", "half a day out"...
#   {biome}      the biome it is in: "the Black Forest"
#   {witness}    who found it; {rteller} who told him; {daysago} "yesterday", "three days back"; {when} "at night",
#                "in daylight", "any time of day"
#
# Firsthand lines say "I". Heard lines name {rteller} or {witness}. Common lines say "they say" or name {witness}.
# Boss lines say what the place is and no more.

# ================================================================== bosses
