# Villheim: conversations of two and three. Same format as lines.txt and chat.txt.
#
# Chat sections can test the other one in the chat (Dialogue.PairTags): with:friend with:rival with:sweetheart
# with:courting with:sametrade with:trade:<x> with:voice:<x> with:child with:jarl with:hurt with:sad with:happy
# with:skilled. {partner} is the one being talked to.
#
# In a chat line, parts are split by "//" and said in turn. A part "@key" is answered by whoever's turn it is, from
# the reply sections tagged answer:key, picked with that villager's own tags and voice (falling back to answer:any).
# An answer can carry its own "//" parts. A part "+key" brings in a third villager standing within 9 m, who says
# a line from the reply sections tagged chime:key.
