# Villheim: players, news, requests and village events. Same format as lines.txt, plus a tone after "~"
# (joke, happy, sad, worry, angry, proud, point, pious) that tells listeners how to react.
#
# Extra tags: player:stranger player:known player:friend player:hero player:new player:disliked
#   news:gift news:defended news:trade news:request   request:open request:done
#   event:stranger event:returning event:request event:requestdone event:gift event:attack event:fightover
# Fill-in: {request} (what the village asked for today, like "20 wood")

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ a stranger walks in

@ talk, event:stranger ~point
Hold on. I don't know your face.
You're new. Where've you come from?
A stranger! We don't get many of those.
Hello there. You're not from round here.

@ talk, event:stranger | gruff ~angry
Who are you, then?

State your business.

@ talk, event:stranger | cheerful ~happy
Oh! A visitor! Welcome, welcome.
You must be tired. Come and sit by the fire.

@ talk, event:stranger | worrier ~worry
Don't come any closer. Sorry. Hello. Are you friendly?
Are you one of the good ones? You look like one of the good ones.

@ talk, event:stranger | elder
Another traveler. Sit down, you'll find we're not much, but we're honest.

@ talk, event:stranger | young ~happy
Who are you? Where are you going? Can I see your sword?

@ talk, event:stranger | devout ~pious
The gods send all sorts. Welcome, traveler.

@ talk, event:stranger | wry ~joke
Ah, a new face. The old ones were getting boring.

@ talk, event:stranger, size:camp
There's only a couple of us so far. You're welcome to the fire.

@ talk, event:stranger, afterfight ~worry
You picked a bad time. We've just had trouble.
