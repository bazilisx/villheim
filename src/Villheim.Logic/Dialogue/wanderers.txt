# Lines for people who belong to no village: pedlars, pilgrims and hermits (Villagers/Wanderers.cs), and what
# villagers say about them. Format as in lines.txt.
#
# Wanderer sections must carry the tag "wanderer"; only those are used for them. Their tags:
#   wanderer  pedlar pilgrim hermit  phase:arrive phase:stay phase:leave  invillage  news
#   morning day evening night  rain cold freezing  biome:meadows biome:blackforest biome:mountain ...
#   healthy hurt badlyhurt
# Channels: greet (on reaching the hearth), bye (leaving), talk (a player talks to them), self (now and then), and
# "talk, sold" after a player buys something.
# Fill-ins as in lines.txt, plus {from} (where a visitor comes from), {news} (the latest from there), {farplace},
# {homeland}, {favfood}, {fear}, {hobby}, {dream}.
#
# Villager sections about visitors use event:pedlar, event:pilgrim and event:blessing (ten minutes after) and
# mem:pedlar, mem:pilgrim (two days after).

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ pedlars

@ greet, wanderer, pedlar
Hello the hearth! Anyone need anything carried a very long way?
Goods from {from}. Fair prices, mostly.
Put the kettle on, I've walked since dawn.
Pedlar! Don't all run off at once.
My feet hurt and my pack is full. Let's fix one of those.

@ bye, wanderer, pedlar
Right. The road won't walk itself.
Keep the fire going. I'll be back when my pack's full again.
If anyone asks, I gave you a good price.
Off I go. Mind the Greydwarfs, they've been bold this year.
Thank you for the seat by the fire.

@ talk, wanderer, pedlar
Shift and talk if you want to see the pack. I don't bite.
I came from {from}. Long way. Lots of trees.
I carry what people don't have and take what they don't want. That's the whole trade.
Everyone wants core wood out here. Nobody wants to go and get it.
You look like someone with coins.
I've slept in nine villages this month and a hollow log twice.
Buy something or tell me a story. Either pays.
A pedlar who says the prices are fair is lying. Mine are fair, though.

@ talk, wanderer, pedlar, news
News from {from}: {news}.
They're talking about it all the way back in {from}. {news}.
You heard? {news}. That's what they told me in {from}.

@ talk, wanderer, pedlar, rain
Rain gets into the pack. Everything I sell smells of wet wool.
Don't stand there in the wet. Come under the eaves and haggle.

@ talk, wanderer, pedlar, night
Bad time to be on the road. That's why I'm here and not there.
I sleep with one hand on the pack. Nothing personal.

@ talk, wanderer, pedlar, phase:arrive
Nearly there. Is that smoke from the hearth?
Hold on, let me get to the fire first.

@ talk, wanderer, pedlar, phase:leave
Too late, the pack's closed. Catch me next time.
Walk with me to the edge of the trees, if you like. No? Fair.

@ self, wanderer, pedlar
Three hides, two resin, one very sad boar tusk.
Where did I put the thistle? I had thistle.
Good village, this. Nobody's tried to rob me yet.
My back. My poor back.
Count it twice, sell it once.
I could stay a week here. I won't, but I could.

@ self, wanderer, pedlar, phase:stay
I'll stay till midday, then on to the next place.
They always want what's at the bottom of the pack.

@ talk, sold, wanderer
Good choice. Don't tell anyone what you paid.
Pleasure. Truly.
That was the last one. It's always the last one.
You won't regret it. Probably.
Spend well, friend.
I'll remember your face. In the good way.

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ pilgrims

@ greet, wanderer, pilgrim
Peace on this hearth. May I sit by it a while?
I'll ask nothing but a place by the fire tonight.
The gods sent me this way. Or the road did. Same thing.
Is there room by the fire for one more?

@ bye, wanderer, pilgrim
Your fire kept me warm. May it keep you safe.
The gods have seen this place. That's my blessing, such as it is.
I go on. Light the fire for whoever comes next.
Rest well, all of you. I'll remember this hearth.

@ talk, wanderer, pilgrim
I walk from shrine to shrine. Most of them are just stones now.
I've come from {from}. I'm going toward {farplace}. After that, who knows.
The dead are all around us here. They don't mind if we're quiet.
Odin hung nine nights on the tree. I can manage one night by your fire.
My pack holds stories, and they're free.
They say this whole land is a place between. I believe it on bad nights.
Every hearth is a little temple, if you squint.

@ talk, wanderer, pilgrim, news
I passed through {from}. {news}. The gods watch over them, I hope.
Last I saw {from}, {news}.

@ talk, wanderer, pilgrim, night
Sit with me. The fire talks if you let it.
This is the hour when the stars are close. Look up.

@ talk, wanderer, pilgrim, morning
Dawn already. I always miss the best part sleeping.
The fire's low. Feed it before you go about your day.

@ self, wanderer, pilgrim
Thank you for the fire.
Hm. A good, honest smoke.
Every road ends at a hearth.
Frigg, keep these people.
My feet have walked further than my mind can follow.

@ self, wanderer, pilgrim, night
The world is very quiet here.
Sleep, little village. I'll keep an eye open.

# ------------------------------------------------------------------ hermits

@ talk, wanderer, hermit
Hm? Oh. A person. It's been a while.
I came out here to get away from people. And here you are.
That's my fire. You can look at it. Don't touch it.
I talk to the trees. They're better listeners than most.
Do you hear that? No? Good. Neither do I.
Sit if you like. I won't make conversation.
I lived in a village once. Too much talking. Too many opinions about my hat.
Out here nobody tells me when to get up.
The ruins over there were somebody's house. Now they're mine. They don't seem to mind.
Shift and talk if you want what I've got. It isn't much.
News can find me if it wants. It never does.

@ talk, wanderer, hermit, biome:blackforest
The Greydwarfs leave me alone. We have an understanding.
Trolls come through here sometimes. I stay very still and think about rocks.
The forest is loud if you listen. Mostly it's saying go away.

@ talk, wanderer, hermit, biome:meadows
It's a soft place, the meadows. Too soft for me, some days.
Boars are good company. They want food and that's it.

@ talk, wanderer, hermit, biome:mountain
Cold keeps the visitors away. Mostly.
Up here the wind does the talking. I just agree with it.
Wolves at night. You get used to it. No, you don't.

@ talk, wanderer, hermit, night
Sit down. You're blocking my fire.
At night the ruins whisper. Old people, old arguments.
I don't sleep much. Someone has to watch the dark.

@ talk, wanderer, hermit, rain
The rain is the only visitor who never asks me anything.
Wet again. My fire is the only dry thing for a mile.

@ talk, wanderer, hermit, hurt
Something bit me. I bit it back. We're even.
Don't fuss. I've had worse from a door.

@ self, wanderer, hermit
Mushrooms by the old wall. Good mushrooms.
Where's my other boot.
Quiet. Good.
More wood on the fire. Always more wood.
Talked to a crow this morning. Smart crow.
I had a name for this place once. Forgot it.
Somebody's walking about. Could be you. Could be worse.

@ self, wanderer, hermit, night
The fire's the only thing out here that needs me.
Stars are out. Hello, stars.

@ greet, wanderer, hermit
Hm.
What do you want?

@ bye, wanderer, hermit
Go on, then.
Mind how you go.

@ talk, wanderer, camper

Ten logs a day back to {from}. Every day. My arms have opinions about it.
It's quiet out here. Too quiet, some nights.

We sleep by the fire and listen for Trolls. Mostly we hear owls.
I volunteered for this. I forget why.

@ talk, wanderer, camper, night

The fire's small on purpose. Big fires bring visitors.

@ talk, wanderer, camper, rain
Rain in the forest is twice the rain. It drips off everything.

@ self, wanderer, camper
More logs. Always more logs.
I miss my bed in {from}.
Was that a branch, or a Greydwarf?
The others back home have no idea how good they've got it.

@ greet, wanderer, camper
Oh, a face. Hello.

@ bye, wanderer, camper
Off you go. Say hello to {from} for us.
