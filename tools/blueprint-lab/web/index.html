Villheim Blueprint Lab

Ask for a building, look at it from any side, check it, and take the .blueprint into the game with PlanBuild. Or load a blueprint you have and strip or furnish it.

Fills in the choices below.

Candidates: the lab builds several seeds and keeps the one that passes the checks and scores highest against your keep and drop review.

Furniture goes against real walls facing in, tables and fires in the middle, banners high on walls, all from where the corpus puts them. Structure is never changed.

Ways to find what is missing

Added pieces show in teal. Each is a guess from the rest of the build; look them over before you take the file into the game. Tested on damaged builds: on symmetric builds it put back about a third of a missing side, and most of what it adds is right.
